Keep its big dark plum eyes, face proportions, cream wool tufts, purple hooves, burgundy red bandana with the white mountain motif, and plum backpack design. New pose: sitting comfortably studying an open dark plum textbook held in its front hooves, looking thoughtfully at the book with a small warm smile. Backpack beside its body. Preserve the original clean softly shaded flat 2D illustration finish and character identity. Full body, all ears and hooves visible, centered and large with a little transparent margin. No humans, no words, no lettering, no background scene, no border, no app UI, no mockup, no extra characters. Genuine transparent alpha background. This is a mascot variation for university students.
